The Quest Begins

On the night before her birthday, a dragon swept out of the sky, captured Akansha, and locked her away in a cage high above the clouds.

But she has something the dragon never counted on: every single friend who has ever loved her.

One by one, each of you must fight your way through the monsters standing between you and her — defeat every enemy, and add your strength to the cause.

When every friend has broken through and reached the dragon... only then can it fall, and Akansha come home to celebrate the birthday she deserves.
